## Dean's Message

#### Prof. Bassant Eyada

Welcome to the College of Media!  
  
Our college is proud to offer two dynamic and accredited programs—Advertising
and Public Relations. Both of our programs have received accreditation from
AQAS, ensuring that we meet the highest standards of education and excellence
in the field.  
  
In today’s fast-paced, media-driven world, the fields of advertising and
public relations are more crucial than ever. We are dedicated to equipping our
students with the skills, knowledge, and experience they need to become
leaders and innovators in these industries.  
  
Our faculty comprises experienced professionals and scholars who are
passionate about teaching and mentoring. They bring real-world insights into
the classroom, ensuring that our curriculum is both relevant and rigorous. Our
programs offer students opportunities for hands-on learning through
internships, industry partnerships, and student-run agencies.  
  
At the College of Media, we emphasize creativity, strategic thinking, ethical
practices, and a global perspective. Our graduates are not just prepared to
enter the workforce—they are prepared to shape it. Whether your passion lies
in creating compelling campaigns, building brand reputations, or navigating
the complexities of media relations, you will find a place here to thrive.  
  
Join us at the College of Media, where your journey to becoming a visionary
leader in the world of media begins. We are excited to welcome you into our
vibrant community, where creativity knows no bounds and every opportunity is
within reach!  
  
Prof. Bassant Eyada  
Dean, College of Media

## Mission

Providing high-quality educational programs that focus on public relations,
advertising and communication, and are consistent with the trend towards the
knowledge economy, the acceleration of technical and information development,
the changing needs of the labor market, and with national and international
educational and professional standards that fulfill students’ desires and
ambitions to reach educational and professional excellence.

![](https://cua-uat.s3.me-central-1.amazonaws.com/wp-
content/uploads/2024/10/fdsFE.jpg)

## Vision

Preparing qualified generations, equipped with communication and humanitarian
knowledge, public relations and advertising skills, technological and artistic
capabilities, and efficiently achieving the developmental goals of the United
Arab Emirates, the Region and the World.
