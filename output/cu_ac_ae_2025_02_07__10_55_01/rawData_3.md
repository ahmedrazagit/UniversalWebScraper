## Dean's Messages

#### Dr. Babeet Gupta

Welcome to City University Ajman's College of Business, supporting a vibrant
lifelong learning environment. The College of Business provides graduate and
undergraduate programs that combine high-quality academic instruction with
hands-on experience in the real world. Our programs will provide you with the
knowledge and skills you need to succeed and excel at work in different
functional areas of Business like Accounting and Finance, Management
information systems, Marketing, Human resource management, Hospitality and
Hotel management. A combination of classroom learning, internships, and
experiential projects ensures that our students are well-prepared to adapt to
new challenges and opportunities in their chosen careers.  
The College of Business continues to be at the cutting edge of innovation and
research. Our academics hold significant leadership and editorial roles, and
they conduct impactful research with an interdisciplinary focus to address
today's challenging business concerns. This attitude of inquiry and
investigation pervades the classroom, improving students' learning experiences
and equipping them to be ethical problem solvers. We're also quite proud of
our faculty, who bring life and energy to our classes.  
I encourage potential students to learn more about how the College of Business
offers a one-of-a-kind educational experience that businesses respect. I
invite new companies to join us in enhancing our students' educational
experience. We accomplish this through a variety of collaboration
opportunities with both our students and faculty. I encourage all of our
students and alumni to get involved and help us build on the successes of the
College of Business at City University, Ajman  
  
Warm regards,  
Dean, College of Business  
Dr. Babeet Gupta

## Mission

To prepare students in the various domains of business administration for
successful professional careers in knowledge-based economies and to assume
leadership roles in dynamic global business environments. By promoting
innovation and entrepreneurship, and collaboration with all stakeholders, the
programs strive to create impact on local and regional communities.

![](https://cu.ac.ae/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/goals5.webp)

## Vision

To be a premier UAE-based International Business School producing
transformational thought leaders capable of contributing to a knowledge-based
economy.
