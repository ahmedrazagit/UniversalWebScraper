## Message from the Head of Department

To our dear Professional Postgraduate Diploma in Teaching students.  
You are about to embark on a rewarding teaching journey filled with impactful
experiences and research-based strategies that will shape not only your
students but also your own growth as an educator. Teaching is a profound
responsibility; it’s about carrying the torch of knowledge and passing it on
to future generations.  
As you step into this role, remember that teaching transcends the confines of
textbooks and lesson plans. It’s about being a role model, embodying values
such as empathy, resilience, and a growth mindset. Your actions and attitudes
will influence how your students view learning and themselves.  
You have the power to instill a love for lifelong learning in your students,
fostering a culture of curiosity and exploration that encourages them to ask
questions, seek knowledge, and embrace challenges with enthusiasm.  
Follow your passion, and continue striving to make a meaningful difference in
the lives of your students.

Dr. Khaled Al Derbashi  
Head of Department
