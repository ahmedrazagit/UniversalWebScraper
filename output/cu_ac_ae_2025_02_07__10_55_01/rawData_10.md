#### Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence

College of Technology offers bachelors in Artificial Intelligence.

[Discover More ](https://cu.ac.ae/academic/bachelor-of-science-in-artificial-
intelligence/)

## Mission

To equip students with cutting-edge skills and knowledge in the rapidly
evolving field of Artificial Intelligence.

![](https://cua-uat.s3.me-central-1.amazonaws.com/wp-
content/uploads/2024/07/jyg.jpg)

## Vision

To offer the best university education in the fields of Artificial
Intelligence sciences, systems, and applications, with international quality
standards, as well as qualifying distinguished cadres to contribute to
community service and development through this field.

![](https://cu.ac.ae/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Untitled-1-min.webp)
