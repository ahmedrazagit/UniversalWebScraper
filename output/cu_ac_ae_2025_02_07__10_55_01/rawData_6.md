## Dean's Message

#### Professor Dr. Ahmed Hassan Foley

Since the law is the safety valve of society, those working in it must make
every effort to develop all of its branches, whether in the civil, criminal,
international, economic, or other fields, to keep pace with these rapid
developments and developments, and to confront the new issues facing our
societies to ensure the sustainability of their progress and progress.  
Dear students  
The College of Law at the City University of Ajman is proud of your
affiliation to it and seeks to prepare you to be able to achieve the lofty
mission of law, by developing your ability to seriously contribute to the
development of all its legislative, judicial and executive aspects, both
substantive and procedural. The College also works to consolidate the
principles and principles of law in you, and to develop... Your ability to
learn Continuous, in-depth research and study that relies on critical thinking
and careful legal analysis of societal issues, their developments, and their
causes, to develop legal systems in their various branches.  
The College of Law is keen to apply the highest standards of academic quality
in the bachelor’s and master’s programs it offers, and is always working to
develop its programs to sharpen the energies of its students and ensure their
excellence. The college includes a group of faculty members who have the
highest degrees of competence and professional experience in the various
sciences of law.  
The College of Law applies an ambitious and realistic strategic vision, the
goal of which is to qualify graduates to compete strongly in the labor market,
by providing them with all theoretical knowledge of the branches of law as
well as refining them with many practical experience skills during their
period of study at the college.  
Professor Dr. Ahmed Hassan Foley  
Dean of the College of Law - City University of Ajman  
  

## Mission

Preparing a generation equipped with specialized legal knowledge in various
branches of law to contribute to community service through students’
acquisition of legal sciences and refining their personal skills to qualify
them to compete in the legal labor market in a way that guarantees them
competence and excellence.

![](https://cua-uat.s3.me-central-1.amazonaws.com/wp-
content/uploads/2024/06/ETSG.jpg)

## Vision

Graduates of legal human resources with high efficiency and distinguished
quality to achieve competitiveness at the local and regional levels.
