# Degrees that get you where you want to go

Open up a world of career opportunities by studying an LIBF degree. Our UK
accredited online degrees are 100% self paced with three yearly intakes in
February, June and October.

At LIBF we equip future leaders with the skills and expertise employers want,
by combining 140 years of heritage with pioneering educational technology.
Join us and take the next step toward progress.

## Higher education in MENA

We focus on helping our participants, members and alumni to progress in their
careers. Although we have a long history, our approach towards learning and
education is industry focused and innovative. Our programmes have been
developed by leading practitioners, to enrich your learning with their
knowledge and experience.

Postgraduate Certificate Banking and Sustainable Finance

The Postgraduate Certificate Banking and Sustainable Finance provides you with
the integration of sustainable practices and principles in the banking and
finance sector and discussed environmental, social and governance (ESG)
factors that are increasingly shaping the finance industry.

[Find out more](https://mena.libf.ac.uk/study/postgraduate-certificate-
banking-sustainable-finance/)

Postgraduate Certificate Banking and FinTech

The Postgraduate Certificate Banking and Fintech equips you with the knowledge
and skills required to navigate the rapidly evolving landscape of the
financial industry, leveraging technological advancements and innovations.

[Find out more](https://mena.libf.ac.uk/study/postgraduate-certificate-
banking-fintech/)

MSc Banking and Finance with specialism

The online MSc Banking and Finance with specialisms in FinTech, Sustainable
Finance or Risk and Compliance will broaden your understanding of modern
banking, whilst addressing the contemporary challenges and opportunities faced
by the industry.

##  Why study with us

We advance careers by equipping people with the skills and knowledge required
by banking and finance roles, to ensure that staff can perform effectively and
responsibly ‐ whilst also shaping the future of the sector. Higher education
programme offering has been designed specifically to meet the demands for the
MENA region within a global context.

Our higher education programmes have been developed by academics and industry
leaders. They've been designed to provide participants with the skills and
knowledge needed for successful careers in the sector. By studying with LIBF
MENA, participants will join a global alumni community of like-minded people
to network, learn and progress in the banking and finance industry.
