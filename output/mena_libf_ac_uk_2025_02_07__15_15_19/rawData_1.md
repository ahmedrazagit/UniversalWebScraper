# Advance your banking and finance career

Our range of flexible qualifications and programmes are designed to develop
your banking knowledge, skills and support you to progress in your role, as
well as career development.

Covering retail, corporate, investment banking, digital banking, fintech,
strategic management and sustainable finance our qualifications and programmes
provide relevant banking and finance education for every stage of your banking
career.

## Learning at every career level

supporting them to perform confidently in their role.

Certificate for Documentary Credit Specialists (CDCS)

The Level 4 Certificate for Documentary Credit Specialists (CDCS)
qualification helps participants develop their documentary credit skills,
along with risk mitigation and control. The course emphasises practical skills
to allow participants to effectively apply their knowledge in the workplace
while dealing with clients.

[Find out more](https://mena.libf.ac.uk/study/certificate-for-documentary-
credit-specialists-cdcs/)

Certificate in Principles of Payments (CertPAY)

The Level 3 Certificate in Principles of Payments (CertPAY) equips
participants with the skills needed to work within the worldwide payments
ecosystem, catering to both the financial and corporate sectors. It
prioritises real-world industry examples and ensures participants gain a
holistic comprehension of payments.

[Find out more](https://mena.libf.ac.uk/study/certificate-in-principles-of-
payments-certpay/)

Certificate in International Trade and Finance (CITF)

The Level 3 Certificate in International Trade and Finance (CITF)
qualification is ideal for individuals responsible for promoting and
delivering trade finance solutions. Participants will gain in-depth insights
into the international trade environment and the role of key stakeholders
involved in international trade finance.

[Find out more](https://mena.libf.ac.uk/study/certificate-in-international-
trade-and-finance-citf/)

Certificate for Specialists in Demand Guarantees (CSDG)

The Level 4 Certificate for Specialists in Demand Guarantees (CSDG)
qualification helps participants develop a comprehensive understanding of
demand guarantees, tender, performance, and advance payment. It also covers
indemnities, suretyships and standby letters of credit, exploring their
nuances and delving into industry rules.

[Find out more](https://mena.libf.ac.uk/study/certificate-for-specialists-in-
demand-guarantees-csdg/)
