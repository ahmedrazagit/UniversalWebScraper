# Department of Biotechnology

## Department of Biotechnology

![Biotechnology](https://aurak.ac.ae/storage/media/biotechnology.jpg)

The Biotechnology Department at AURAK offers a comprehensive four-year
undergraduate program covering diverse areas of Biotechnology, such as
Molecular and Cellular Biology, Genetics, Drug Discovery, Medical
Biotechnology, Forensics, and Food and Agricultural Sciences. The program
combines expert-led courses with practical laboratory sessions, including co-
requisite laboratory-based modules, providing theoretical knowledge and
practical skills.

Offering a solid foundational knowledge in Mathematics and Chemistry, advanced
biotechnology courses, and substantial practical training, our Biotechnology
program prepares students for careers in the pharmaceutical industry,
healthcare industry, forensics laboratories, food industry, research, and
education. The Biotechnology Department features six fully equipped
laboratories that allow students to carry out hands-on and experiential
activities. The laboratories have state-of-the-art facilities for cell
culture, molecular biology, light and fluorescence imaging, protein analysis,
DNA analysis, and microbiology, providing students with hands-on experience
with cutting-edge equipment and techniques.

Step into the world of Biotechnology through our esteemed program and unlock
countless prospects through our diverse program majors. You’ll have an
exciting path to explore, enabling you to seize ample opportunities and embark
on a thrilling journey of growth and success.
