# Department of Accounting & Finance

## Department of Accounting & Finance

The Department of Accounting and Finance at AURAK is dedicated to preparing
students for successful careers in accounting and finance.

The department offers locally and internationally accredited majors. Courses
are taught by internationally qualified faculty, covering various topics,
including financial accounting, managerial accounting, auditing, taxation,
corporate finance, financial markets, and investment analysis.

At the Department of Accounting and Finance, the faculty is committed to
providing a student-centered learning experience that helps the students to
acquire the knowledge and skills necessary to excel in their chosen careers.
Join AURAK, and benefit from a comprehensive education that will prepare you
for the challenges of the innovative and dynamic global marketplace in
accounting and finance.

The department offers two majors; Bachelor of Science in Business
Administration – major in Finance, and Bachelor of Science in Business
Administration – major in Accounting.
