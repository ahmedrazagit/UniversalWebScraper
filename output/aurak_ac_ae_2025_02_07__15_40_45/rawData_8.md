# Department of Humanities and Social Sciences

## Department of Humanities and Social Sciences

The Department of Humanities and Social Sciences at AURAK offers courses in
diverse fields of study, including Arabic, communication, education, fine
arts, history, philosophy, psychology, sociology, Islamic studies, and world
civilization. Working closely with the Department of Mathematics and Natural
Sciences, we are committed to providing students with a strong liberal arts
education that enhances critical thinking, communication, and problem-solving
skills, preparing them for various careers and further studies.

Our faculty comprises leading experts in their respective fields who provide
an outstanding education to our students. We take pride in our supportive
learning environment that fosters the exploration of passions and interests,
facilitating personal and intellectual growth.

Join us at the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences and gain the
skills and knowledge necessary to succeed in the 21st century.
