Astra Tech is a UAE-based technology investment and development group,
embarked on a mission to build the world’s first ultra platform & ecosystem,
that will disrupt the consumer technology space by rethinking the boundaries
of a digital ecosystem.

  

It will be the first of its kind in the MEA region to simplify the way people
& businesses interact, engage, and transact with each other. The platform will
provide seamless connectivity between consumers and businesses, enabling users
to effortlessly access home, e-commerce, and fintech services in one place.
Astra’s mission is to enrich people’s lives by making communication and
e-commerce more seamless, connected, and natural.

  

**_Key Responsibilities_**

  

  * Responsible for daily review of databases, performance monitoring, deployment of alerts, and fault diagnosis.
  * Installation and deployment of database servers, perform simple performance optimization, ensure safe operation of databases, etc.
  * Assist the team in achieving the availability, optimization of performance, troubleshooting, design capacity, and construction of the database platform of the company's database services.
  * Take the initiative to learn and understand the cutting-edge technologies in the database industry, and be able to apply them to the company, control the overall direction of the company's internal database technology evolution and iteration.
  * Create a good technical atmosphere in the team.

  

**_Experience Required_**

  

  * Understanding of basic database storage engines (such as InnoDB, MyISAM, NDB) functions and features, as well as the principle and practice of master-slave replication.
  * Basic understanding of MySQL, TiDB, Redis, and ES.
  * Basic knowledge of SQL query optimization techniques and related database tools.
  * Basic scripting language programming ability and familiarity with one or more of the following languages: Python, Shell, Perl, Java, Go.
  * Strong sense of responsibility, good teamwork spirit, excellent communication and presentation skills.
  * Good ability to handle pressure and respect production systems.
