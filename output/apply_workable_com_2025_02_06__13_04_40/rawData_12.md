**About the Company:** We are a fast-growing SaaS startup revolutionizing the
professional services industry with a powerful platform powered by cutting-
edge technology. Our mission is to enable professionals to work smarter and
faster by delivering innovative, user-focused solutions. If you are passionate
about building and maintaining robust data systems and thrive in a fast-paced
environment, join us to make an impact!

  

  

**About the Role:** As a Data Engineer, you will play a pivotal role in
building and maintaining reliable data pipelines, handling diverse datasets,
and creating critical integrations with industry-standard Document Management
Systems (DMS). Your expertise in Python, data engineering, and DMS integration
will be essential in ensuring seamless interoperability between our platform
and client systems, including NetDocuments, iManage, SharePoint, and eDocs.
This role requires prior experience with DMS integration work, as it will be a
primary focus of your responsibilities.

  

  

**Responsibilities:**

  

  

  * DMS Integration Development: Design, develop, and maintain integrations with client Document Management Systems, including NetDocuments, iManage, eDocs, and SharePoint, ensuring seamless connectivity and data flow.
  * Data Pipeline Development: Build and maintain robust data pipelines to support live and batch processing of data across the platform.
  * Data Scraping & Cleaning: Extract and preprocess data from websites and other sources, ensuring it is clean, accurate, and usable.
  * OCR Implementation: Leverage Optical Character Recognition (OCR) tools to extract information from unstructured and scanned documents.
  * Multilingual Data Handling: Process and manage datasets in multiple languages to meet diverse client needs.
  * System Maintenance: Monitor, troubleshoot, and optimize data systems for performance, reliability, and scalability.
  * Collaboration: Work closely with product managers, developers, and stakeholders to deliver data solutions aligned with business requirements.

  

  

**Qualifications:**

  

  

  * DMS Integration Expertise: Proven experience developing integrations with NetDocuments, iManage, eDocs, SharePoint, or similar Document Management Systems.
  * Data Engineering Skills: 3+ years of professional experience in building and maintaining data pipelines.
  * Programming Skills: Proficiency in Python, with a strong focus on scalable and maintainable solutions.
  * Web Scraping & Cleaning: Hands-on experience with tools such as BeautifulSoup, Scrapy, or equivalent frameworks.
  * Database Skills: Strong knowledge of SQL (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and experience working with large datasets.
  * OCR Tools: Familiarity with OCR technologies such as Tesseract, Amazon Textract, or similar.
  * Cloud Platforms: Experience working with AWS, GCP, or Azure for data storage and processing.
  * Multilingual Data Processing: Ability to handle datasets in various languages effectively.

  

  

**Preferred Skills:**

  

  

  * Knowledge of ETL processes and big data tools (e.g., Apache Airflow, Spark).
  * Experience with DevOps tools such as CI/CD pipelines, Docker, and Kubernetes.
  * Familiarity with professional services workflows and industry standards.

  

  

**Ideal Candidate:**

  

  

  * DMS Integration Specialist: Strong background in building integrations with leading Document Management Systems.
  * Data Pipeline Expert: Skilled at creating efficient and reliable data pipelines.
  * Problem-Solver: Excels at addressing data challenges with innovative solutions.
  * Collaborative Team Player: Thrives in cross-functional teams and fast-paced environments.

  

  

**What We Offer:**

  

  

  * Competitive salary and benefits package.
  * Opportunity to work on industry-transforming technology.
  * A dynamic startup culture that values innovation and ownership.
  * Professional growth and learning opportunities.
  * Flexible work arrangements to suit your lifestyle.

innovative solutions for the professional services industry!
