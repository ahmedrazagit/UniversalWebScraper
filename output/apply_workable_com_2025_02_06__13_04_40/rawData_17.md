**Database Tsar (Data Acquisition & Integration Lead)**

**Location:** Dubai & Abu Dhabi

**Company:** Prepaire Labs

  

**About Us**

Prepaire Labs is on a mission to revolutionize personalized medicine through a
next-generation, AI-powered GenetiQ Digital Twin. We integrate multi-omics
data, advanced analytics, and real-world insights to deliver personalized
healthcare recommendations.

  

**Role Overview**

As the Database Tsar, you will scout, evaluate, and integrate new data sources
to enhance our predictive models and knowledge graph. Your role goes beyond
daily database management; you will design and optimize the entire data
pipeline. If you are passionate about data quality, interoperability, and
enabling AI-driven insights, this position is for you.

  

**Key Responsibilities**

  * Identify, research, and evaluate external datasets, including genomic, proteomic, and clinical data, to enhance the knowledge graph and AI models.
  * Design and optimize pipelines for ingesting diverse data sources, including APIs, bulk uploads, and streaming data.
  * Select, deploy, and maintain database solutions, including SQL, NoSQL, and graph databases.
  * Implement best practices for data validation, compliance, and privacy.
  * Collaborate with engineering, data science, and DevOps teams to ensure seamless data flow and fast iteration cycles.

  

**Qualifications**

  * Five or more years of experience in data engineering, database administration, or a related role.
  * Expertise in both SQL and NoSQL databases; familiarity with Big Data pipelines such as Spark and Kafka is a plus.
  * Experience working with life sciences or healthcare datasets is strongly preferred.
  * Strong communication skills with the ability to manage external partnerships and negotiate data usage rights.

  

**What We Offer**

  * The opportunity to work on meaningful, high-impact projects at the intersection of AI and healthcare.
  * A collaborative environment that values creativity, innovation, and problem-solving.
  * Flexible working arrangements, including remote and hybrid options.
  * Competitive compensation and opportunities for professional growth.

  

**How to Apply**

We value results and innovation. If you are ready to shape the future of
personalized medicine, send your resume to **tajana.d@prepaire.com**
