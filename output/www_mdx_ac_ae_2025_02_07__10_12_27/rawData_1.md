# Postgraduate Programmes

Scroll down for more information

##

Middlesex University Dubai’s admissions policies are based on the principle of
wider participation – MDX believes that everyone should have the opportunity
to pursue a University degree. Middlesex does not discriminate on the basis of
race, colour, or national or ethnic origin in admitting students to its
programmes.

All students who are interested in a programme of study at Middlesex
University are required to meet our academic requirements and English language
requirements.

##  Entry Requirements for Postgraduate Programmes

###  Academic Requirements – Postgraduate

We accept students with a diverse range of entry qualifications and from
varying backgrounds. Specific academic and work experience requirements exist
for each of our postgraduates programmes as shown in the table below:

## Name of Programme

|

## Academic and Work Experience Requirements  
  
---|---  
  
###### MBA (Master of Business Administration)

|  Normally a second class UK honours degree or above, or an equivalent
qualification and a minimum of 3 years professional full-time work experience.
Candidates may be eligible without an honours degree if they can demonstrate a
significantly successful managerial experience**.  
  
###### MA Human Resource Management

|  Normally a second class UK honours degree or above, or an overseas
equivalent qualification. Applicants without a degree who have practical
experience in personnel work will also be considered, but you will need to
demonstrate your ability on the basis on courses already attended, references
supplied, a 2,000-word essay and an interview.  
  
###### MA Marketing Communications

|  Normally a second class UK honours degree or above, or overseas equivalent
qualification. Applicants without a degree who have minimum 3 years managerial
experience in sales or marketing will also be considered.  
  
###### MSc Applied Psychology

|  We welcome applications from graduates with a good honours degree, or
equivalent qualification, in an appropriate subject. We also consider
candidates with other relevant qualifications and individuals with a minimum
of three years’ work experience.  
  
###### MSc Computer Network Management

|  A second class honours degree (UK) or an equivalent overseas qualification
in computer science or networks, or in a field that provided significant
exposure to IT, for example, management, business, mathematics, science or
engineering. We also accept applicants with degrees in other fields who can
demonstrate relevant industrial experience combined with extensive IT
background.  
  
###### MSc Project Management

|  Applicants will be expected to have a good honours degree (2nd class
Honours or above) or equivalent in an engineering based discipline. Graduates
from other related disciplines may also be admitted to the programme after
interview. Preference will be given to graduates with industrial experience.
In addition candidates will have such qualities as being creative, proactive
and having a desire to engage with the curriculum, and be able to think as an
individual but able to work in a team. Candidates should be able to show a
keen interest in engineering in all its aspects.  
  
_** Managerial experience implies managerial responsibility for people and/or
functions and/or technical expertise, e.g. accountancy._  
_Potential candidates are encouraged to contact our Admissions Office for
individual consultation on a case-by-case basis._
