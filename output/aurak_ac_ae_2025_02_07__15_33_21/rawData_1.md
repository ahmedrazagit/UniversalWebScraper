# Department of Architecture

## Department of Architecture

![Architecture small \(1\)](https://aurak.ac.ae/storage/media/architecture-
small-1.jpg)

The Department of Architecture at AURAK is committed to providing a
specialized education focused on design proposals and building technology
implementation in both built and natural environments.

Our curricula are designed to meet the highest standards of educational
quality in the field and have been crafted in accordance with the National
Architecture Accreditation Board (NAAB) (for BArch) and with Council for
Interior Design Accreditation (CIDA) (for BAID) standards.

Our Architecture program provides students with a comprehensive education on
creating sustainable built environments, and Interior Design trains students
in creating healthy, user-friendly interior spaces.

Students from both programs learn to use various design tools and techniques,
including hand and digital drawing, and the most advanced software for design
modeling and 3D visualization.

****

Our programs prepare students for careers in architecture and interior design,
where digital design skills and knowledge of building technologies are
essential. Join us at the Department of Architecture and gain the skills and
knowledge necessary to excel in these exciting and dynamic fields.

## Facilities

The Architecture Department at AURAK has a digital laboratory equipped with
state-of-the-art equipment to support teaching and research activities in the
main areas of construction and design. Architecture Digital lab contains 18
PCs. Each PC is equipped with AutoCAD 2016; Revit 2016; 3ds Max 2016; Rhino 5
for windows, ADOBE Photoshop, ADOBE Indesign and CutePDF Writer. All computers
are connected to Canon iPF785 plotter (max. paper width: 950 mm), Océ (Plot
Wave 360) and Universal Laser System (Work Space size 80 cm x 45 cm).

  * **Architecture studios** : four studios equipped with workstations, lockers, drawing board with tools, drawing stand and data show
