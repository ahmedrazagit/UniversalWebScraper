# Department of Mathematics and Physics

## Department of Mathematics and Physics

The Department of Mathematics and Physics at AURAK offers a range of courses
in chemistry, mathematics, and physics. Our department provides students with
a strong foundation in these subjects, which are critical for success in
various fields. Through our rigorous coursework and hands-on laboratory
experiences, we aim to prepare our students for the challenges of the modern
world and equip them with the skills necessary for success in their chosen
careers. At AURAK, we recognize the importance of a well-rounded education
with a strong liberal arts foundation.

Working closely with the School of Engineering, the Department of
Biotechnology and the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences, we provide
a broad-based approach to learning that develops linkages between the
humanities, social sciences, natural sciences, and engineering. This approach
is designed to produce graduates who are knowledgeable in their fields of
study and possess the critical thinking and communication skills necessary for
success in any career. Join us today and become part of a dynamic community of
scholars and researchers dedicated to advancing the frontiers of knowledge and
tackling the challenges of the 21st century.
