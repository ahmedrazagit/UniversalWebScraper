# Department of Civil and Infrastructure Engineering

## Department of Civil and Infrastructure Engineering

![SAKI1245](https://aurak.ac.ae/storage/media/saki1245-1.jpg)

The Department of Civil and Infrastructure Engineering at AURAK provides a
comprehensive education to our students in civil and infrastructure
engineering. Our four-year undergraduate program is unique in the region. The
CIEN program’s curriculum is designed to equip its graduates with the
necessary skills to embark upon their role as future civil engineers towards
the region’s development.

Structural engineering, geotechnical engineering, environmental engineering,
transportation and pavement engineering, and construction engineering and
management are the core subspecialties of the CIEN program. The program
addresses society’s most pressing challenges, including sustainable
engineering, global issues, and multidisciplinary problem-solving .

In addition, our program is designed to closely align with the current and
future direction of civil and infrastructure engineering professions in the
21st century. The curriculum covers various topics, including construction and
management, structural and materials engineering, highway and transportation
engineering, geotechnical engineering, and water and environmental
engineering. Our state-of-the-art laboratories, equipped with the latest
instruments provided by Stevin Rock L.L.C., allow our students to gain hands-
on experience in various areas, including surveying, materials testing,
geotechnical analysis, and more.

Upon graduation, our students are well-prepared for careers in various fields,
including engineering design, planning, consulting, and contracting firms.

Join us at the Department of Civil and Infrastructure Engineering and become
part of a vibrant and dynamic community of engineers dedicated to improving
society through innovative and sustainable solutions .

### [Bachelor of Science in Civil and Infrastructure Engineering
](https://aurak.ac.ae/undergraduate-degrees/engineering/bachelor-of-science-
in-civil-and-infrastructure-engineering)

  

The Civil and Infrastructure Engineering Department at AURAK has several
laboratories equipped with state-of-the-art equipment to support teaching and
research activities in the main areas of civil engineering.  

**Construction material lab:**

Contain equipments for testing characteristics of different construction
materials including vicat apparatus for testing cement paste, compressive and
tensile strength testing machine for testing mortar and concrete and UTM
machine to test tensile strength of steel rods. It also contains all
equipments and tools required to prepare, mix, mold, cut, drill and cure
concrete.

CIEN 332: Construction Materials lab

[General Safety Rules](https://aurak.ac.ae/files/aurak/SOE/Laboratory-general-
safety-rules-stevin-1.pdf)  

**Fluid Mechanics and Hydraulics Lab**

Equipped with a flume, multi-Pump test rig, computer controlled wind tunnel,
pipe surge and water hammer apparatus, fluid friction measurements apparatus
and a range of smaller fluid mechanics apparatuses.  

CIEN 334: Civil Engineering Testing and Materials

[General Safety Rules](https://aurak.ac.ae/files/aurak/SOE/Laboratory-general-
safety-rules-stevin-2.pdf)  

**Surveying lab**

Contains a range of surveying equipment including Total station, Theodolite
and Digital level. **  
**

CIEN 261: Surveying

[General Safety Rules](https://aurak.ac.ae/files/aurak/SOE/Laboratory-general-
safety-rules-stevin-2.pdf)  

**Geotechnical Engineering lab**

contains equipment to prepare, mix and test soil samples to determine the
strength of soil. Main equipments are direct shear, consolidation and Triaxial
testing machines.

CIEN 334: Civil Engineering Testing and Materials

[General Safety Rules](https://aurak.ac.ae/files/aurak/SOE/Laboratory-general-
safety-rules-stevin-4.pdf)  

**Highway lab**

contains state of the art equipment to prepare, mix and test asphalt binders
and asphalt hot mixes; Marshall and Superpave Mix Design. Different Asphalt
Binder grading and Asphalt Hot Mixes performance tests can be conducted using
Dynamic Shear Rheometer (DSR) and the Dynamic (Cyclic) Universal Testing
Machine.

CIEN 334: Civil Engineering Testing and Materials

[General Safety Rules](https://aurak.ac.ae/files/aurak/SOE/Laboratory-general-
safety-rules-stevin-5.pdf)  

**Advanced Computing Central Facility**

equipped with 30 PCs and a plotter to train students on AutoCAD, ArcGIS and
other engineering softwares.

CIEN 201: Computer Aided Drawing

[General Safety Rules](https://aurak.ac.ae/files/aurak/SOE/Laboratory-general-
safety-rules-stevin-6.pdf)
