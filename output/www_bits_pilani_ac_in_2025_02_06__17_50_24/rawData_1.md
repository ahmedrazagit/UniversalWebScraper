#### Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.)

Engineering programmes are mathematics and hard science based that incorporate
many up-to-date techniques of analysis and synthesis. These degree programmes
are available with both Practice School and Thesis/Seminar options with a
normal duration of 4 years.

[ KNOW MORE __](https://www.bits-dubai.ac.ae/admissions/be/)

![Master of Engineering \(M.E.\)](https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/wp-
content/uploads/M.E.png)

#### Master of Engineering (M.E.)

The M.E. programmes of 2 years of normal duration are uniquely designed and
structured for Engineers who are employed professionals or in the pursuit of
seeking employment. These programmes give an immersive professional experience
and an opportunity to acquire further competence either in one’s own
discipline or gain complementary knowledge in Engineering as well as
Interdisciplinary areas to upgrade their career.

[ KNOW MORE __](https://www.bits-dubai.ac.ae/admissions/me/)

![Master of Business Administration\(M.B.A.\)](https://www.bits-
pilani.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/MBA.png)

#### Master of Business Administration(M.B.A.)

The MBA programme at BITS Pilani, Dubai Campus aimed for graduates across
various disciplines, working professionals and entrepreneurs offers core
management courses with specialized electives focusing on real-world industry
practice. Students graduating with this MBA degree acquire a dynamic ability
to apply methodical, technical and innovative thought processes to business
problems in their respective fields.

[ KNOW MORE __](https://www.bits-dubai.ac.ae/admissions/mba/)

![Doctor of Philosophy \(Ph.D.\)](https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/wp-
content/uploads/Ph.D.png)

#### Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.)

Research is an integral component in an institute of higher learning. BITS
Pilani Dubai campus with its state-of-the-art research facilities offers Ph.D.
programmes with strong emphasis laid on interdisciplinary, mission oriented
and relevant research. Topics of Research can be chosen from any of the
disciplines in which the Institute offers the First and Higher Degree
Programmes or any interdisciplinary or allied areas.
