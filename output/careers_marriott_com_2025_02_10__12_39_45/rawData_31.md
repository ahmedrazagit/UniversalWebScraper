## Job Description

**Join e & NAFIS Programme **

Are you ready to embark on an experience that will hone your skills and
enhance your role in the private sector, in collaboration with the Emirati
Talents Competitiveness Council? At e&, we’re not just embracing the future;
we’re driving it—and we want you to join us.

This programme is pivotal to our mission: to develop UAE National talent into
the next generation of leaders across various fields. You’ll explore emerging
technologies, gain valuable leadership skills, and acquire hands-on experience
through placements across e& offices in the UAE. The programme is designed to
prepare you for roles that meet the evolving requirements of the private
sector.

**Why Join NAFIS at e &?**

At e&, we offer UAE Nationals the opportunity to sharpen their skills and
enhance their competencies, boosting their competitiveness in the private
sector.

Our diverse range of roles spans several departments, including Sales,
Technology, Business, Operations, and Customer Service. This variety ensures
that there is a suitable opportunity for every candidate, regardless of their
background or area of interest.

**What’s the programme about:**

  * **Tracks:** Sales, Technology, Business, Operations, Customer Service, and more.
  * **Learning:** You'll have access to top-learning and development programs through our best-in-class educational partners.

**What we offer:**

  * **Vibrant Culture:** Work in a people-centric organization with a diverse team of highly motivated professionals that unite as one, dare to be bold, and are customer-obsessed.
  * **Competitive Benefits:** Enjoy a competitive salary package with perks like medical insurance, our exclusive 50% discount "Freedom" packages.
  * **Work-life Balance:** Experience our flexible work environment that perfectly balances your professional and personal life.

**Who Should Apply:**

  * **Education** : High school diploma, Bachelor's degree accredited by the UAE Ministry of Education (open to all majors).
  * **Experience** : Recent graduates or professionals with up to 2 years of experience.
  * **Passion** : We are looking for motivated and enthusiastic individuals ready to contribute to our digital future.

**Diversity is our strength:**

At e&, we cherish diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive
environment for all. We encourage people from all backgrounds, ages, and
abilities to apply. If you require any adjustments or accommodations during
the selection process, please reach out to
[DiversityEquity&Inclusion@eand.com](mailto:DiversityEquity&Inclusion@eand.com)
