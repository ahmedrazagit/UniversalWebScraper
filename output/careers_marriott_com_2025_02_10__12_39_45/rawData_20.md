### Intern, Media Partnerships

Media Partnerships (MPNR) - Dubai, Dubai

Apply for This Job

**The Company******

**Asia Football** Group (AFG) is the exclusive commercial agency for the Asian
Football Confederation’s (AFC) competitions from 2023-2028. Our partnership
with the AFC covers the commercial rights for all major AFC National Team and
Club competitions, including the AFC Asian Cup, AFC Asian Qualifiers, AFC
Women’s Asian Cup, AFC Women’s Olympic Qualifiers, AFC Champions League, AFC
Cup as well as AFC youth and futsal competitions.

Driven by a shared passion and dedication to Asian football, our industry
experts have a wealth of experience in global, regional, and local football
marketing, and are devoted to delivering exciting and engaging Asian football
content to fans on ground, on screen, and online. Working closely with the AFC
and its stakeholders, Asia Football Group strives for the success and growth
of Asian football, as well as redefining Asian football’s commercial standing
within global football by addressing the shared business objectives of the
AFC, its sponsors and media partners with solutions derived from optimising
fan engagement and digitally driven innovations.

**The Role**

The Media Partnerships Intern will work in the media partnerships team of the
media and marketing services department. Acting as a key team-wide coordinator
supporting a wide range of administrative, operational, and client management
activities, working in a global broadcast environment delivering Asia’s
biggest and most celebrated football competitions and tournaments.

The role requires an organised, personable, and proactive individual with an
eye for detail who enjoys working in a dynamic business environment. An
eagerness to learn and a positive attitude would be the key to success in this
role.

**Key Responsibilities******

  * General team administration, internal communication, and project management 
  * Support the team on various logistical matters, including supporting the delivery of media rights at AFC events 
  * Manage relationships with certain vendors and other AFG departments
  * Support the development of internal and external documents (e.g., presentations, overviews, workshops, reports, etc.)
  * Conduct diverse ‘desk research’ projects to support client servicing
  * Coordinate client entertainment and relations programs (E.g., event hosting and hospitality, client workshops)
  * Work collaboratively with staff, partners, suppliers, and other involved parties

****

**Skills, Experience and Qualifications******

  * Prior experience or degree in sponsorship/sports marketing is a distinct advantage
  * University degree
  * Interest and knowledge in football is an advantage.
  * Passion for footballs/ sports, media, entertainment and/or broadcast
  * Ability to manage multiple tasks at a time
  * Emphasis on timelines and delivery of projects under pressure
  * Knowledge of design tools like Photoshop, Illustrator, etc. is a distinct advantage
  * Excellent verbal and written communications skills in English (additional fluency in other languages within AFC region a distinct advantage – particularly Arabic)
  * Highly proficient in Microsoft Office (PowerPoint, Word, Excel, and Outlook)
