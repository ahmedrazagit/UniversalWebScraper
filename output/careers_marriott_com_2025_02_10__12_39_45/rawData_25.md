# Administrative Support & Customer Communication Intern

  *   * Full-time

  * Legal Entity: Robert Bosch Middle East FZE

## Company Description

Do you want beneficial technologies being shaped by your ideas? Whether in the
areas of mobility solutions, consumer goods, industrial technology or energy
and building technology with us, you will have the chance to improve quality
of life all across the globe.

Welcome to Bosch.

## Job Description

  * Accurately manage and update spreadsheets.
  * Draft, format, and proofread professional letters and emails.
  * Respond promptly to customer inquiries and provide support.
  * Assist with basic technical support for audio and conference systems.
  * Perform administrative tasks, including scheduling meetings and preparing documents.
  * Collaborate with cross-functional teams on projects and initiatives.

·**Benefits:**

  * Gain hands-on experience in a global corporate environment.
  * Develop technical skills related to audio and conferencing tools.
  * Receive mentorship from experienced professionals.
  * Access potential career advancement and networking opportunities.

## Qualifications

  * Currently enrolled in a relevant Bachelor's degree program (e.g., Business Administration, Communications, Engineering).
  * Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint (experience with Google Workspace is a plus).
  * Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
  * Basic understanding of audio and conference systems.
  * Previous experience in customer service or administration is a plus.

**Personal Attributes:**

  * Young, energetic, and eager to learn.
  * Detail-oriented with strong multitasking abilities.
  * Proactive, professional, and adaptable.
  * Excellent communication skills with a strong focus on customer service.
