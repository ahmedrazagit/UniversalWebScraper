### Description

At PwC, we measure success by our ability to create the value that our clients
and our people are looking for. Our reputation lies in building lasting
relationships with our clients and a focus on delivering value in all we do.
We’re a network of firms in 158 countries with more than 236,000 people who
are committed to delivering world-class capabilities and quality in assurance,
tax and advisory services.

Established in the region for 40 years, PwC Middle East employs over 4,200
people across 12 countries. Complementing our depth of industry expertise and
breadth of skills is our sound knowledge of local business environments across
the Middle East region. Our tailored solutions help our clients meet the
challenges and opportunities of doing business in the Middle East market and
beyond.

Internal Firm Services

**Job Description & Summary**

Summary  
Join our Emerging Technology Lab as an Intern, where you will have a unique
opportunity to learn about and help shape the future of technology at PwC. You
will work alongside experienced professionals to explore how emerging
technologies can solve real business challenges for our clients. Through
hands-on projects, mentorship, and collaboration, you will gain practical
skills and insight into how we identify, prioritize, and implement innovative
solutions.

Responsibilities  
Research and Development  
Assist in ideation and brainstorming of potential prototypes to address PwC’s
emerging challenges.  
Support research on emerging technologies relevant to PwC’s industry.  
Help evaluate the potential impact of new technologies on PwC’s business
model.  
Contribute ideas for innovation and emerging technology adoption.  
Planning  
Assist in planning project phases and timelines under the guidance of senior
team members.  
Track and follow up on prototype development milestones to ensure timely
delivery.  
Identify and communicate potential issues or risks to supervisors for
resolution.  
Provide updates on project status to stakeholders, team members, and lab leads
as needed.  
Technical Skills  
Learn and develop skills in front-end web development, databases, and version
control (Git).  
Work with React or Next.js, as well as Tailwind CSS, under the supervision of
senior developers.  
Gain exposure to emerging technology tools (e.g., programmatic use of LLMs,
machine learning, computer vision) as opportunities arise.  
Documentation and Reporting  
Assist with documentation of prototype development processes, including
design, development, testing, and deployment.  
Help maintain accurate records of project timelines, budgets, and resources.  
Support the generation of reports and insights from prototype data for the
team and clients.  
Lab and Team Support  
Help set up prototypes in the lab and ensure the space is organized for client
visits.  
Collaborate with the team to address prototyping challenges and brainstorm new
ideas.  
Provide basic technical support to team members and interns working on their
own prototypes.  
Communicate effectively with stakeholders, team members, and external clients.  
Contribute to a culture of innovation and continuous learning within the lab.  
Adhere to best practices and standards for emerging technology development as
established by the team.
