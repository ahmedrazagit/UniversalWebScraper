**OUR EXPECTATIONS FROM THE ROLE:**

  * Follow strict personal hygiene procedures to ensure safe food handling.
  * Adhere to food safety policies, including HACCP guidelines and Cold Chain compliance.
  * Prepare and cook food according to recipes, maintaining quality and presentation standards.
  * Monitor food quality during preparation using HACCP forms and production charts.
  * Safely and appropriately use kitchen tools and equipment, ensuring proper handling and engagement of safety devices.
  * Use measuring tools to precisely measure ingredients and portion sizes.
  * Report work-related accidents or injuries promptly.
  * Follow company, hotel, and department safety and security policies and procedures.
  * Adhere to Marriott International Hotels Limited Regional Office policies and procedures.
  * Actively listen and respond positively to guest questions, concerns, and requests.
  * Anticipate and address guests' service needs professionally and promptly.
  * Communicate effectively with guests and co-workers, using clear and appropriate language.
