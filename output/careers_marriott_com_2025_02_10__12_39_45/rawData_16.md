#  2025 ZWS Internship Program - UAE

121007

## Job Summary

Zurich Workplace Solutions (ZWS) is an entity from Zurich International
located in the heart of the DIFC’s International Financial Centre. They
provide localized workplace savings solutions that offer flexibility, control
and make a positive difference to long-term financial security.

ZWS are the Administrator of the DIFC Employee Workplace Savings plan (DEWS),
a progressive end-of-service benefit plan introduced within the DIFC in 2020.

Be part of a purposeful, values driven, global organization with meaningful
and impactful work. There are many reasons why Zurich is the right choice for
you. But, perhaps, the most important reason is the opportunity you will have
to grow and thrive in your career. Working with global, diverse and inclusive
teams, you’ll have the chance to develop valuable skills, explore your
passions and make a positive impact beyond your role - Let’s Grow Together.

## **The Exposure**

You will have access to many trainings and expand your network by interacting
with different colleagues and teams, taking the best out of your journey with
us. The internship will provide you have an opportunity to work in either of
the following areas (**please select your preferred choice**).

**Customer Service Consultant:**

• Get hands on training on our end of service product, services, tasks, and
systems.  
• Get an opportunity to interact with customers via calls, emails, and web
chats.  
• Meet customer requirements through processing a range of tasks encompassing
the full product range.  
• Identify, build, and maintain excellent customer relationships and loyalty
by supporting our customers.  
• Gain and demonstrate knowledge of relevant systems, products, processes, and
procedures to aid delivery of seamless customer service to our clients.  
• Learn to take personal responsibility for resolving customers concerns.  
• Get a chance to work collaboratively across all Operational areas.  
• Demonstrate a flexible and co-operative approach towards changing business
needs.  
• Get trained on how to be professional and accountable for all your
responsibilities and actions.  
• Provide feedback and recommendations for improvement of customer service
processes and systems to manager and colleagues.

**Financial Operations Officer**

• Learn how employer contributions are tracked and reconciled against system
and bank account.  
• Gain insight into importance of accurate contribution allocation and how
discrepancies are managed.  
• Understand how excess and shortfall contributions are handled with
processing of refunds.  
• Understand how contributions are invested in a timely manner to learn how
SLAs are maintained.  
• Work with internal teams to understand the end to end contribution and
investment processes.  
• Learn how to address queries in relates to contributions and investments.
Thus, to understand the role of clear communication in resolving financial
discrepancies.  
• Understand the reporting requirements for contributions and investments.  
• Gain exposure to compliance considerations in financial operations.  
• Gain and demonstrate knowledge of relevant systems, products, processes, and
procedures to aid delivery of seamless customer service to our clients.  
• Learn to take personal responsibility for resolving customers concerns.  
• Get a chance to work collaboratively across all Operational areas.  
• Demonstrate a flexible and co-operative approach towards changing business
needs.  
• Get trained on how to be professional and accountable for all your
responsibilities and actions.  
• Provide feedback and recommendations for improvement of customer service
processes and systems to manager and colleagues.

## **The Learning Outcome**

**Customer Service Consultant**

**What you will learn through this opportunity**

• Enhance your communication and interpersonal skills.  
• Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines.  
• Polish your attention to detail and accuracy.  
• Customer service orientation and problem-solving skills.  
• Working in a fast-paced environment and multi-tasking.

**Financial Operations Officer**

**What you will learn through this opportunity**

• Gain hands-on experience in financial operations  
• Develop an analytical mindset and problem-solving skills.  
• Enhance your communication and interpersonal skills.  
• Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines.  
• Polish your attention to detail and accuracy.  
• Customer service orientation and problem-solving skills.  
• Working in a fast-paced environment and multi-tasking.

## **The Candidate**

**Basic Criteria**

  * Valid residence visa within the United Arab Emirates
  * Open to fresh graduates or anyone who graduated within the past 12 months

**Customer Service Consultant**

• Good communication and interpersonal skills  
• Basic computer skills and familiarity with Microsoft Office applications.  
• Willingness to learn and develop new skills.  

**Financial Operations Officer**

• Fresh graduates in Finance, Accounting, or related field.  
• Strong analytical thinkers and problem-solving skills.  
• Basic computer skills and familiarity with Microsoft Office applications.  
• Willingness to learn and develop new skills.
