As one of the UAE’s leading technological institutions, RIT Dubai is a
vibrant, connected community that is home to diverse, ambitious, and creative
students from more than 75 countries.

RIT Dubai offers an incredible array of academic programs; a diverse,
committed, and accessible faculty; sophisticated facilities; and an unusual
emphasis on experiential learning.
