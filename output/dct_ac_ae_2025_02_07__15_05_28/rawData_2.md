EVENT MANAGEMENT

Given Dubai's unprecedented growth, the city has evolved into an epicenter for
high-profile events. With the range of jobs and career prospects on offer in
Dubai, a qualification from DCT will present you with exciting career
prospects.

The key to any successful event is organisation, planning, and execution. Our
one-year Certificate programme combines theory and practical education to
empower you in these areas.

Upon completion of the Certificate programme, students can continue their
studies with our two-year Advanced Diploma in Events Management to give
graduates the tools required to master all facets of Event Management.

Course Structure

YEAR ONE | Certificate 4 for Events Coordinators

25,000 AED

  * Level 4 (NQF)
  * Credit Hours: 68
  * Duration: One academic year
  * Study Mode: Full-time
  * Intake: Autumn (September)

During the one-year Certificate 4 in Events Coordinators programme students
study core units which provide the fundamental business and academic skills
required in any Events related position such as: cultural awareness and
workplace communication, service skills fundamentals and marketing.

Event students will also study specialised units providing them with tailored
content that prepares them for working within Dubai’s events industry.

For more information on all units, click [here](/media/l4khxtz3/master-module-
document_-certificate-4-for-event-coordinators.pdf "Master Module Document
Certificate 4 For Event Coordinators")

Students are also placed on a mandatory 6-week internship as part of the
Certificate Level 4 programme.

*Accredited by the Ministry of Education (MOE). Valid until 28.09.2027.

YEAR TWO & THREE | Advanced Diploma in Event Management

25,000AED/Year

  * Level 6 (NQF)
  * Credit Hours: 132
  * Duration: Two academic years
  * Study Mode: Full-time
  * Intake: Autumn (September)

Upon successful completion of the certificate level 4, students can choose to
progress to the Advanced Diploma in Event Management programme, which requires
an additional two years of study. The Advanced Diploma focuses on teaching
students more supervisory, teamwork and leadership skills. You will have the
opportunity to complete a longer industry internship placement of 4 months in
Year 2, and another 4 months in Year 3.

For more information on all units, click [here](/media/ppcj210c/master-module-
document_-l6-advanced-diploma-in-event-management.pdf "Master Module Document
L6 Advanced Diploma In Event Management")

Students study subjects within Event Management such as, event supplier
coordination, event organizational tools and digital marketing.

**_Please note:_**

**_On successful completion of the qualifications, Certificates and Advanced
Diplomas will be issued per policy._**
