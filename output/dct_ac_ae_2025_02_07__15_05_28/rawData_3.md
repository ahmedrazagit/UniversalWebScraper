HOSPITALITY

Dubai is home to some of the world's most iconic hotels and is fast developing
a reputation for luxury and service excellence. This sector provides
incredible opportunities for those with an eye for detail and a flair for
service.  
  
Our one-year Certificate programme combines theory and practical education to
equip graduates with key skills required to succeed in the industry such as
food and beverage service, front office operations and service skills
fundamentals.  
  
Upon completion, students can continue their studies with our two-year
Advanced Diploma in Hospitality Management in order to master all facets of
Hospitality Management.

Course Structure

YEAR ONE | Certificate 4 in Hospitality Operations

25,000 AED

  * Level: 4 (NQF)
  * Credit Hours: 68
  * Duration: One academic year
  * Study Mode: Full-time
  * Intake: Autumn (September)

During the one-year Certificate 4 in Hospitality Operations programme students
study core units which provide the fundamental business and academic skills
required in any hospitality related position such as: cultural awareness and
workplace communication, service skills fundamentals and marketing.

Hospitality students will additionally study specialised units providing them
with more in-depth knowledge and a deeper understanding of the key hospitality
departments and roles.

For more information on all units, click [here](/media/xjfjyqsi/master-module-
document_certificate-4-in-hospitality-operations.pdf "Master Module Document
Certificate 4 In Hospitality Operations")

Students are also placed on a mandatory 6-week internship as part of the
Certificate Level 4 programme.

*Accredited by the Ministry of Education (MOE). Valid until 28.09.2027.

YEAR TWO & THREE | Advanced Diploma in Tourism and Hotel Management

25,000AED/Year

  * Level: 6 (NQF)
  * Credit Hours: 132
  * Duration: Two academic years
  * Study Mode: Full-time
  * Intake: Autumn (September)

Upon successful completion of the certificate level 4, students can choose to
progress to the Advanced Diploma in Tourism and Hotel Management programme,
which requires an additional two years of study. The Advanced Diploma focuses
on teaching students more supervisory, teamwork and leadership skills. You
will have the opportunity to complete a longer industry internship placement
of 4 months in Year 2, and another 4 months in Year 3.

Students will study subjects such as rooms division management, food and
beverage revenue management, leading teams and digital marketing.

For more information on all subjects, click [here](/media/vqmoi3wu/master-
module-document_-l6-advanced-diploma-in-tourism-and-hotel-management.pdf
"Master Module Document L6 Advanced Diploma In Tourism And Hotel Management")

**_Please note:_**

**On successful completion of the qualifications, Certificates and Advanced
Diplomas will be issued as per policy.**
