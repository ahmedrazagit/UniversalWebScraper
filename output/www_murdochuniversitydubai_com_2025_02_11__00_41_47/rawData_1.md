## Choose your course

At Murdoch, we offer a comprehensive range of degrees and majors, providing
you with the opportunity to specialise in an area (or two) of your choice.

All our undergraduate degrees have 24 units (72 credit points) and follow the
same course structure and integrity as the programs at our home campus in
Perth.

At Murdoch University you also have the option to choose double majors with no
additional fees or duration added to your course.
