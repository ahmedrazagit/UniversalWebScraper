## Take your career to the next level

Go further with our flexible and industry relevant postgraduate programs. You
can begin studying our postgraduate degree once you have an undergraduate
degree. Or if it’s your first time at uni, you can apply with significant
industry experience that you’ve gained throughout your career.

Postgraduate degrees can deepen your understanding and knowledge of your
speciality area or undergraduate degree or they can help you switch career
paths and embark on a brand new journey in a different area.

Murdoch University Dubai offers postgraduate degrees at two different levels
depending on your career goals and aspirations – Graduate Certificate and
Master.
