# Courses

At Curtin Dubai, you can choose from a wide range of courses offered in
Foundation, Undergraduate, and Postgraduate studies.

[Home](https://curtindubai.ac.ae "Go to Home.")Courses

Curtin University is a global university recognised for innovation in
teaching, learning experiences, and delivery methods that are highly
responsive to the changing needs of industry and careers.

Whether you’re starting your first degree, or progressing your career; it all
starts with a world-class degree from Curtin. There are many different study
areas at Curtin Dubai. Whether you’re looking to study a three or four years
bachelor’s degree, Curtin Dubai offers you courses that suit your needs.
